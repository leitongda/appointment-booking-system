# 就诊服务域

> 就诊相关服务，包括病历、医生端操作、停诊调班等

- 基础路径: /api/v1
- 认证方式: Bearer Token (JWT)

本域包含 3 个模块，共 31 个端点。

---

## 病历

- [GET /api/v1/medical-records](/api/v1/medical-records): 获取病历列表
- [POST /api/v1/medical-records](/api/v1/medical-records): 创建病历
- [GET /api/v1/medical-records/my](/api/v1/medical-records/my): 获取当前医生的病历列表
- [GET /api/v1/medical-records/patient/{patient_id}](/api/v1/medical-records/patient/{patient_id}): 获取患者的病历列表
- [GET /api/v1/medical-records/appointment/{appointment_id}](/api/v1/medical-records/appointment/{appointment_id}): 根据预约ID获取病历
- [GET /api/v1/medical-records/{record_id}](/api/v1/medical-records/{record_id}): 获取病历详情
- [PUT /api/v1/medical-records/{record_id}](/api/v1/medical-records/{record_id}): 更新病历
- [DELETE /api/v1/medical-records/{record_id}](/api/v1/medical-records/{record_id}): 删除病历

## 医生端

- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/me](/api/v1/doctor-portal/me): 获取当前医生信息
- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/schedules](/api/v1/doctor-portal/schedules): 获取当前医生的排班列表
- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/schedules/today](/api/v1/doctor-portal/schedules/today): 获取当前医生今日排班
- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments](/api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments): 获取当前医生的预约列表
- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments/today](/api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments/today): 获取当前医生今日预约
- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue): 获取当前医生的签到队列
- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/info](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/info): 获取当前医生的叫号队列统计信息
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments/{appointment_id}/call](/api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments/{appointment_id}/call): 叫号 - 只能叫自己的患者
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments/{appointment_id}/complete](/api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments/{appointment_id}/complete): 完成就诊 - 只能操作自己的患者
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments/{appointment_id}/pass](/api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments/{appointment_id}/pass): 过号 - 只能操作自己的患者
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments/{appointment_id}/recall](/api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments/{appointment_id}/recall): 重新叫号 - 只能操作自己的患者
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments/{appointment_id}/no-show](/api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments/{appointment_id}/no-show): 标记爽约 - 只能操作自己的患者
- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/statistics/today](/api/v1/doctor-portal/statistics/today): 获取当前医生今日统计数据
- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/patients/{patient_id}/appointments](/api/v1/doctor-portal/patients/{patient_id}/appointments): 获取患者在当前医生处的历史就诊记录
- [POST /api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments/{appointment_id}/follow-up](/api/v1/doctor-portal/appointments/{appointment_id}/follow-up): 为患者创建复诊预约

## 停诊调班

- [GET /api/v1/leave-requests](/api/v1/leave-requests): 获取申请列表（管理端）
- [GET /api/v1/leave-requests/pending-count](/api/v1/leave-requests/pending-count): 获取待审核数量
- [GET /api/v1/leave-requests/my](/api/v1/leave-requests/my): 获取我的申请列表（医生端）
- [GET /api/v1/leave-requests/{request_id}](/api/v1/leave-requests/{request_id}): 获取申请详情
- [POST /api/v1/leave-requests/stop-clinic](/api/v1/leave-requests/stop-clinic): 创建停诊申请
- [POST /api/v1/leave-requests/schedule-change](/api/v1/leave-requests/schedule-change): 创建调班申请
- [POST /api/v1/leave-requests/{request_id}/review](/api/v1/leave-requests/{request_id}/review): 审核申请
- [POST /api/v1/leave-requests/{request_id}/cancel](/api/v1/leave-requests/{request_id}/cancel): 取消申请
