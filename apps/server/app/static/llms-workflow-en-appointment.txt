# Appointment Booking Workflow

> Complete workflow for patients from viewing doctors to completing appointment booking

- Base Path: /api/v1
- Authentication: Bearer Token (JWT)

---

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1: View Department List

- [GET /api/v1/departments/tree](/api/v1/departments/tree): 获取科室树

### Step 2: View Doctors in Department

- [GET /api/v1/doctors/by-department/{department_id}](/api/v1/doctors/by-department/{department_id}): 获取科室医生

### Step 3: View Doctor Details

- [GET /api/v1/doctors/public/{doctor_id}](/api/v1/doctors/public/{doctor_id}): 获取医生详情(公开)

### Step 4: Get Available Schedules

- [GET /api/v1/schedules/available/{doctor_id}](/api/v1/schedules/available/{doctor_id}): 获取可预约排班

### Step 5: Get Booking Notices

- [GET /api/v1/booking-notices/public](/api/v1/booking-notices/public): 获取生效预约须知列表

### Step 6: Check Duplicate Appointment

- *No matching endpoints*

### Step 7: Create Appointment

- [POST /api/v1/appointments](/api/v1/appointments): 创建预约

### Step 8: View Appointment Details

- [GET /api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/payment-params](/api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/payment-params): 获取支付参数
- [GET /api/v1/appointments/query](/api/v1/appointments/query): 获取预约列表
- [GET /api/v1/appointments/query/{appointment_id}](/api/v1/appointments/query/{appointment_id}): 获取预约详情
- [GET /api/v1/appointments/query](/api/v1/appointments/query): 获取预约列表
- [GET /api/v1/appointments/query/{appointment_id}](/api/v1/appointments/query/{appointment_id}): 获取预约详情

### Step 9: Cancel Appointment

- [PUT /api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/cancel](/api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/cancel): 取消预约
- [PUT /api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/cancel-sign-in](/api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/cancel-sign-in): 取消签到
- [PUT /api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/cancel-sign-in](/api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/cancel-sign-in): 取消签到
