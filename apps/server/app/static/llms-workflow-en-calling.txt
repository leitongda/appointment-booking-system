# Doctor Calling Workflow

> Workflow for doctor portal calling and visit management

- Base Path: /api/v1
- Authentication: Bearer Token (JWT)

---

## Workflow Steps

### Step 1: Get Check-in Queue

- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue): 获取叫号队列
- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue): 获取叫号队列

### Step 2: Get Queue Statistics

- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/info](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/info): 获取队列信息
- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/info](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/info): 获取队列信息

### Step 3: Call Patient

- [PUT /api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/call](/api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/call): 叫号
- [PUT /api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/call](/api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/call): 叫号
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/call](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/call): 叫号
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/call](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/call): 叫号

### Step 4: Complete Visit

- [PUT /api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/complete](/api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/complete): 完成就诊
- [PUT /api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/complete](/api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/complete): 完成就诊
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/complete](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/complete): 完成就诊
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/complete](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/complete): 完成就诊

### Step 5: Pass Patient

- [PUT /api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/pass](/api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/pass): 过号
- [PUT /api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/pass](/api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/pass): 过号
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/pass](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/pass): 过号
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/pass](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/pass): 过号
- [PUT /api/v1/tenants/{tenant_id}/password-policy](/api/v1/tenants/{tenant_id}/password-policy): 更新租户密码策略

### Step 6: Recall Patient

- [PUT /api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/recall](/api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/recall): 重新叫号
- [PUT /api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/recall](/api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/recall): 重新叫号
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/recall](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/recall): 重新叫号
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/recall](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/recall): 重新叫号

### Step 7: Mark as No-show

- [PUT /api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/no-show](/api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/no-show): 标记爽约
- [PUT /api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/no-show](/api/v1/appointments/{appointment_id}/no-show): 标记爽约
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/no-show](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/no-show): 标记爽约
- [PUT /api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/no-show](/api/v1/doctor-portal/queue/{appointment_id}/no-show): 标记爽约

### Step 8: View Patient History

- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/patients/{patient_id}/appointments](/api/v1/doctor-portal/patients/{patient_id}/appointments): 获取患者历史
- [GET /api/v1/doctor-portal/patients/{patient_id}/appointments](/api/v1/doctor-portal/patients/{patient_id}/appointments): 获取患者历史

### Step 9: Create Follow-up Appointment

- [POST /api/v1/doctor-portal/patients/appointments/{appointment_id}/follow-up](/api/v1/doctor-portal/patients/appointments/{appointment_id}/follow-up): 创建复诊预约
- [POST /api/v1/doctor-portal/patients/appointments/{appointment_id}/follow-up](/api/v1/doctor-portal/patients/appointments/{appointment_id}/follow-up): 创建复诊预约
