# Clinical Service Domain

> Clinical services including medical records, doctor portal operations, and schedule adjustments

- Base Path: /api/v1
- Authentication: Bearer Token (JWT)

This domain contains 3 modules with 35 endpoints.

---

## 病历

- [GET /api/v1/medical-records](/api/v1/medical-records): 获取列表
- [POST /api/v1/medical-records](/api/v1/medical-records): 创建病历
- [GET /api/v1/medical-records/my](/api/v1/medical-records/my): 获取我的列表
- [GET /api/v1/medical-records/patient/{patient_id}](/api/v1/medical-records/patient/{patient_id}): 获取患者列表
- [GET /api/v1/medical-records/appointment/{appointment_id}](/api/v1/medical-records/appointment/{appointment_id}): 根据预约获取
- [GET /api/v1/medical-records/{record_id}](/api/v1/medical-records/{record_id}): 获取详情
- [PUT /api/v1/medical-records/{record_id}](/api/v1/medical-records/{record_id}): 更新病历
- [DELETE /api/v1/medical-records/{record_id}](/api/v1/medical-records/{record_id}): 删除病历
